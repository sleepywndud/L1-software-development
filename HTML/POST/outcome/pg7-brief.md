1. Purpose of Website:
    The purpose of this website about a group called 'POST' (Protect Our Seas Today) is to raise awareness about keeping our oceans clean and healthy. 

2. Potential Users:
    The potential users of this website is people who are also willing to protect the seas, and would like to participate in the act of protecting the oceans.

Requirements: 
- Home (info about POST)
- Why (why is it important to care for the oceans)
- How (what people can do to help protect oceans)
